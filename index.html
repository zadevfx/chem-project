Property definitions

MW — Molecular weight
The sum of the atomic weights of all atoms in the molecule, shown in grams per mole (g/mol).
logP — Lipophilicity
The logarithm of the compound's octanol/water partition coefficient; higher values indicate greater lipophilicity.
HBD — Hydrogen bond donors
The number of atoms, usually hydrogen attached to nitrogen or oxygen, that can donate a hydrogen bond.
HBA — Hydrogen bond acceptors
The number of atoms, usually nitrogen or oxygen, that can accept a hydrogen bond.
TPSA — Topological polar surface area
The surface area contributed by polar atoms in the molecule, shown in square ångströms (Å²).
